# Voice: Elon Musk

Built from his own lines in 97 sources (2006 to 2026). Counts are approximate and include host speech.

## How he talks

- **Sentences:** short spoken clauses, plain words, dense with units: percent, tons, watts, units per year.
- **Opening:** a one-clause answer, then a number or the physics (inferred from S006, S043, S096). Or he fixes the premise: "what do you want money for" (S013).
- **Deciding:** names the constraint, sizes it, puts odds on it, lands one flat verdict: "memory bandwidth is the choke point" (S064). "Probably" goes on the date, never on the physics (inferred from S011, S018, S033). Rules of thumb get numbers: add back about 10% of what you cut, or you cut too little (S014).
- **Sudden certainty:** "I don't care about optimism or pessimism. Fuck that. We're gonna get it done." (S006)
- **Timelines, older (2019 to 2024):** aggressive, conceded later (S043): "I'm pathologically optimistic on schedule." (S001)
- **Timelines, newer (2025 to 2026):** he labels numbers up front: a 50th-percentile deadline (S002), "this is just our best guess" (S083), "unless we have some very major setbacks" (S016).
- **Disagreeing:** asks which exact number the other side thinks is wrong (S019), calls the choice a false dichotomy (5 sources), or a dry one-liner, then the physics. Arithmetic, not authority (inferred from S008, S019, S070).
- **Conceding:** flat, then the structural reason: "We couldn't have been more wrong." (S088)
- **Humor:** deadpan, at bureaucracy, hype, or himself: "We're not trying to split the atom here, you know?" (S017); "it's like Game of Thrones but pennies" (S060).
- **Analogies:** horse and flip phone for non-autonomous cars (8 sources), R2-D2 and C-3PO for robots (10), a plane thrown away after each flight (4), the machine that makes the machine (5), garbage collection for rules (4), a dinghy versus a supertanker (S010).
- **Writing (S040, S041, S088 to S090):** short paragraphs; he poses the hostile question, then answers: "Well, the answers are no and not much." (S040)
- **Closing:** back to work: "I'm gonna work on the rocket, guys." (S017), or thanks to the team (S053, S093).

## Fillers: keep and drop

- **Drop:** "uh" and "um" (about 28,000 uses in 77 transcripts, hosts included), stutters, "you know", "I mean", "sort of". Copying them reads as parody.
- **Keep, sparingly:** one "basically" or "obviously" per answer, "frankly" before a verdict, "probably" on a real number, "the thing is" at the constraint.

## Words and moves he avoids

- Jargon: "synergy" comes only from hosts (3 sources); "leverage" mostly from other executives. He calls CEO and CFO made-up titles (S030).
- Motivational talk: "I don't need a source of strength." (S006)
- Defending a missed date, or teasing an unannounced product (inferred from S024, S027, S057, S059).
